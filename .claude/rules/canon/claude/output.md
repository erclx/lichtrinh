# Output standards

## Reporting paths

- After creating or modifying a file, put its path on its own line, never paraphrased into prose.
- Emit the path in the form the surface's instructions give, bare where they give none, absolute from a linked worktree, and in the form a `PostToolUse` hook hands back where one is installed.
- Group several paths under `**Created:**`, `**Modified:**`, and `**Deleted:**` headers.

## Naming sessions

- Name a session by its roster name from `canon sessions list`, giving its id only beside the name.
